# Design System: Flow Circle

**Philosophy**: Deep Calm. Soft Shapes. No Hard Edges.

## 🎨 Color Palette ("Deep Space Calm")

### Backgrounds (Dark Mode Base)
*   `--color-bg-deep`: `#0F172A` (Slate 900 - The Void)
*   `--color-bg-card`: `#1E293B` (Slate 800 - The Platform)
*   `--color-bg-subtle`: `#334155` (Slate 700 - The Shadow)

### Accents (Soft Pastels)
*   `--color-primary`: `#C084FC` (Soft Purple - Flow State)
*   `--color-secondary`: `#818CF8` (Soft Indigo - Focus)
*   `--color-success`: `#34D399` (Soft Emerald - Achievement)
*   `--color-text-main`: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100 - Clarity)
*   `--color-text-muted`: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400 - Context)

### ⛔ Forbidden Colors
*   Pure Black (`#000000`) - zu hart.
*   Neon Green (`#00FF00`) - zu aggressiv.
*   Pure Red (`#FF0000`) - zu alarmierend.

## 🔤 Typography
*   **Font Family**: 'Outfit' (Sans-Serif, Geometric, Friendly)
*   **Weights**: 300 (Light), 400 (Regular), 600 (SemiBold).
*   **Sizes**: Large Headers, comfortable Body text (16px+).

## 📐 Shape & Motion
*   **Radius**: `rounded-3xl` (24px) for cards, `rounded-full` for buttons.
*   **Animation**: `ease-out`, `duration-500` (Langsam & Fließend). "Breathing" effects statt Blinken.
